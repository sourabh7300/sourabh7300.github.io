04 — Layers

Stack it like a pro.

Real layer compositing: stack images, text and overlays, set blend modes and opacity, drag them into place. Everything is non-destructive.

  • Blend modes — Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Soft light, Add and more.
  • Overlays — light leaks, lens flare, dust and scratches, film border.
  • Text and images — drop in a logo or a caption, move and rotate it.
  • Layer history — undo any move, blend or delete.

05 — Batch

A folder, one click.

Pick a whole shoot. Lucid applies your look to every photo, resizes, adds your watermark and hands you a single ZIP.

  • Resize — set a width, keep the aspect.
  • Watermark — text, corner, size and opacity.
  • PNG, JPEG or WebP — with a quality slider.
  • One ZIP — built in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

06 — Sense

Capture. Edit. Instantly.

The SENSE camera bridge opens your camera inside the studio. Take a frame and it lands straight on the canvas, ready for curves, looks and layers. Or drop it in as a layer.

  • Camera to canvas — no export, no import.
  • Capture as layer — composite a live shot over your photo.
